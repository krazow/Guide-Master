Put Guide Master on your Home Screen

On iPhone and iPad it takes four taps. Then the game opens full screen, like an app, and plays offline.

The steps in a 24-second loop. Tap the video to pause it.

You’re already playing from your Home Screen. Nothing else to do: tap Back to the game.

Step by step

  1. Open the game in Safari (tap Back to the game).
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up). On iPhone it’s in the toolbar at the bottom; on iPad, at the top right. If you don’t see the toolbar, tap the address bar or the ⋯ button first.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen. Not there? Tap View More or Edit Actions… at the end of the list to find it.
  4. Leave Open as Web App on, if you see it, and tap Add at the top right.
  5. Find the Guide Master icon on your Home Screen and tap it. The game opens full screen.

Using Chrome, Edge or Firefox? On iOS 16.4 and later you can add it from their Share menu too (Chrome and Edge: the Share button in the address bar or menu, then Add to Home Screen). If it isn’t there, open this page in Safari.

It works the same on iPhone and iPad. On iOS 16.4 and later you can also do it from the Share menu in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

Playing offline

Open the game once from the new icon while you have a connection. It saves itself on your phone, and from then on it plays anywhere: in airplane mode, on the river, with no signal. When there’s an update, it picks it up the next time you’re online.

Your saved games

Important: on iPhone and iPad, the Home Screen app keeps its own saves, separate from Safari. A career started in Safari doesn’t show up in the app, and the other way round.

So start (or continue) your career from the Home Screen icon.

Already playing in Safari? Move your career over:

  1. In Safari, on the game’s title screen, tap 🔁 Transfer a saved game, then 📋 Copy code.
  2. Open Guide Master from the Home Screen icon and tap 🔁 Transfer a saved game.
  3. Paste the code into the box and tap Load pasted code. Your career carries on in the app.

The code holds the save in the current slot; repeat for each slot you want to bring along.
